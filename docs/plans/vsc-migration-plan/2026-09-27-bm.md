## G7 — A live, unresolved upstream proposal would remove the member D4 relocates

**Evidence.** cred-spec [#58](https://github.com/trustoverip/dtgwg-cred-spec/issues/58)
(OPEN, checked 2026-09-27), *"Carry the trust-task citation in committed form:
`taskContext` and `taskDigestMultibase` are durable correlators (ZKP record
008)."* The Trust Tasks editor (`stormer78`) has proposed a different
resolution from the one the issue opened with: *"the credential stops carrying
`taskContext` and `taskDigestMultibase` at all. The issuer signs a separate,
small citation document that binds the credential (by digest) to the exchange
… No blinding value, no ZK, and nothing in the credentia[l]…"* Brendan
(`bmiller59`) commented favorably — *"a nice piece of design work … gets you
both properties that matter … without asking the holder to manage a new
secret"* — without adopting it as settled.

**Why this lands on D4 specifically.** Plan §3 D4 is *"`taskContext` moves
from inside `credentialSubject` to the top level."* If #58's detached-citation
proposal is adopted instead, `taskContext` and `taskDigestMultibase` do not
move — they are **removed from the credential entirely**, replaced by a
separate signed document. That is a bigger and differently-shaped change than
a relocation, and it also touches `witnessCeremony.ts`'s task-binding check
(§3.2), which currently reads these members off the credential.

**What it does not touch.** D6 (the subject-issuer binding) and D8 (predicate
rejection) are about the *witnessed edge*, not the task citation, and are
unaffected either way. Nothing in V0–V3 depends on where `taskContext` sits or
whether it exists on the credential at all — verification-first work proceeds
unchanged. Only V4's D4 sub-step is conditional on this.

**Position adopted.** Flag D4 in §3 as conditional on #58's outcome; re-check
before V4 starts that sub-step. Recorded as plan §9 Q7. Not blocking V0–V3.
