**taskContext must bind, not just locate.** Glenn: a document id is
reusable — "it's not proof of anything"; anchor on the proofValue (slight
preference) or a hash; try both. Tried both as **ref-06w3** (13 checks): the
forgery (counterfeit session document, genuine id) is *accepted* under
id-only; a digest over the JCS form (excluding `proof`) rejects it
unconditionally at ~5 µs, proofed or not; proofValue is spoofable as a string
match (copy the proof block verbatim), only binds behind full signature
verification (which costs more than the digest), and cannot exist on the
unproofed sessions `witness/session` 0.1 permits (`proofRequirement.request:
OPTIONAL`). **Verdict: `taskContext` stays the id (locator) + REQUIRED
`taskDigestMultibase` (binder).**
