### Overlaps, and what is not a subset

- No leg is a strict subset of another. The overlaps are in setup: the
  approved join through `run-join-waiting.mjs` runs with rows in `waiting` and
  as setup in `update-android` and `relaunch-android` (and `agents` joins C
  through its own helper for R2 and R10); the R1 second-agent step runs with
  rows in `agents` and as setup in `update-android`, `relaunch-android` and
  `push2`.
- `relaunch-android` and `update-android` share a 15-minute setup; their
  verdict parts differ (install-over and `update-community-kept` against
  force-stop, `update-persona-used` and `relaunch-keys-fetched-on-use`).
  `relaunch-android.sh:2` says the plain relaunch is deliberate ("the project lead's
  239 report without the update").
- `linkresume` and `linkwait` have the same script shape (one fresh install
  per `LINK_CASE`), split by case duration and by the "beside iOS" slot
  (`gate.sh:32-34`).
- `testreq` and `p1`'s approvals both approve a held request with the #317
  owner check, through different rules (`vta/contexts/create/1.0` set from
  the phone; an approver set on `vta/contexts/get/1.0` from the host).
- `smoke-ci-ios` is a subset of `smoke-ios` by design; `smoke-ci-android` is
  not a subset of `smoke-android` (it adds the welcome rows and counts token
  lines by pid).
- Not covered by any leg: the Farm one-scan setup (`run-farm-onescan.mjs`

### What `gate.sh changed` maps

`changed.mjs` maps changed files to legs through the testID owners' manifests
(`lib/testids.json`, `lib/testids.app.json`) and the drivers' reach from each
leg's `node` invocations (`changed.mjs:23-62, 135-244`). A changed file under
`bifold/packages/core/src` or `app/src` that owns no testID is "unmapped" and
forces the full gate (exit 2); a changed file anywhere else (drivers, page
objects, native folders, patches, other bifold packages, workflows) is
silently ignored, and a fix in those alone yields exit 1 ("write a row").
Rows printed by shell rather than by a driver (the plain-launch rows,
`kk-link`, `update-no-missing-key`) are invisible to it.

## 3. The positions this plan takes, and the ones it rejects

- **Eliminate legs outright.** Rejected: no leg is a subset of another (§1);
  what is duplicated is setup, so the plan removes setup (one linked phone
  per lane) and merges the two legs whose setup is identical.
- **Emulator snapshots for the shared phone.** Rejected: `-no-snapshot-save`
  still loads the quickboot snapshot and discards the session's state on
  shutdown, which ate the 226 gate's Android preparation on 28 September
  (memory `emulator-snapshot-eats-state`). The plan keeps the emulator
  running, cold-booted with `-no-snapshot`.
- **`adb backup` and restore to clone a linked phone.** Rejected: the
  wallet's keys live in the Keystore and do not travel with app data.
- **Shorten the clocks in the shipped build, or at runtime from the
  Developer screen.** Rejected for the shipped build (nothing baked in; the
  store configuration is what testers get). A Developer-screen override was
  considered and set aside: it would ship in the store build as latent
  behaviour. A build-time switch in test builds keeps the store build
  untouched; the test build already differs from it by configuration (push
  off and on), and `tier.mjs`'s fingerprint sees no native change.
- **Shorten `linkfail`'s hold.** Not possible from the app: the 181 s is the
  agent's consent wait on the VTA. The lever is the lane (run it beside other
  work), not the clock.
- **Detox or Maestro instead of Appium.** Rejected: Detox needs a Detox-built
  binary (a precompiled native dependency added to the app), and the gate
  installs the artifact testers get; Maestro has no physical iOS support and
  would be a second toolchain beside 57 drivers; Appium with testIDs is what
  Shopify took to 98% stability (§4). The gate's locators are already testID
  based (1,618 calls, no XPath; parent plan, proposal B).
- **Cloud device farms for the lanes.** Rejected in the parent plan
  (metered minutes at this rerun cadence; the runner tool's keys in the Mac's
  keychain). The lanes are the Mac, the Mac mini and GitHub's free runners
  with the CI identity that went green on 14 October.
- **Two lanes on one Farm runner.** Rejected by the coordinator's rule of 14
  October: two identities on one runner collide on its ACL and rate limit.
  The lanes need runner VTAs of their own; this is the one Farm ask in the
  plan.
- **Counting `p1-tui-vets` as an application failure.** The records label it
  `app`; the evidence (six reds, two greens, same pins, the TUI's 3-second
  reads) says timing on the TUI side. The plan reports it as env until the
  TUI driver waits on state.
