## Context

The sprint's screens (RoomsHome, RoomOffer, RoomView, the attachment sheet) were built as bifold
#597, #601, #602, #636, #637, #638 and #640. A screen-by-screen comparison against the data rooms
mock the sprint followed (v1, 2026-10-25) found the mock had more than the sprint's screen table:
- a Rooms section on each community's screen;
- a room's Files, Notes and Members as parts of the room;
- rooms in the Messages list;
- a confirmation before each send.

Three of these needed a decision. Alberto answered them on 2026-10-27.

## What was decided

1. **Each community's screen has a Rooms section**, beside membership and vetting. It lists that
   community's offers and the member's rooms there. The Rooms card on the agent home stays: it is
   still the one place that lists every room, since rooms are not tied to one community.
   **Supersedes** the sprint's "A per-community entry on `VtiCommunity` is not discoverable",
   which ruled the community screen out as an entry. It is now a second way in, not the only one.
2. **Rooms do not appear in the Messages list in this sprint.** They stay under My Agent, as the
   sprint has them. To revisit after the real-community leg (S0.3 and S2's live list) works:
   a Messages row for a room needs a feed of new posts, which today is pull-only and per room.
3. **No confirmation on each send.** The composer always shows who the member posts as
   ("Posting as …"), above the field. That answers the mock's open question 4 (confirming every
   message in a busy room): the identity is in view at the moment of posting, and nothing interrupts
   the send.

## Also adopted from the mock, needing no decision

**A room's parts as tabs under its header:** Chat, Files, Notes and Members. These are a row of
tabs inside RoomView, not a navigator, so a draft and a running transfer survive a switch (the
sprint's reason for keeping rooms out of the bottom tabs applies here too).
- **Files** is the room's library (`listFiles`), newest first, opening the attachment sheet.
- **Notes** holds the room's titled records and the markers of removed ones. A record with neither
  a title nor a removal stays in Chat, shown as written. A `keyring.chat/1` message has no
  title, so Keyring's own posts are always in Chat.
- **Members** shows the member's own access (what they may do, until when, as which identity),
  the community's list where the member may see it (`listCommunityMembers`, status-gated), and
  leaving the community.

A member being added or removed shown in the chat (the mock's fourth flow) waits on a
membership-event feed, which the rooms service doesn't have.
