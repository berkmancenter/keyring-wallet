### Conflicts and resolutions

**C1. Maestro/Detox bake-off.** My scratch revisions proposed running Maestro, Appium and possibly Detox for a week and choosing Maestro for single-device smoke. The project lead decided on 2026-10-14 that Appium stays (decision 8, `2026-10-14-al.md` §5), and the gate subtask records the reasons. Resolution: dropped; recorded in plan §8 as a rejected alternative with its constraints and a reopen condition (driver-attributable flake in T0's ledger). The prior-art research's vendor flake figures (Detox under 2%, Maestro under 1%, Appium 10 to 20%) were never usable evidence and are not cited.

**C2. Persona snapshots.** The branch plan's E2 builds golden personas from an emulator quick-boot snapshot and a cloned simulator. The gate subtask §4 rejects emulator snapshots after the 226 gate lost its Android preparation to `-no-snapshot-save`, and instead keeps one emulator running with a ledgered linked phone. Resolution: the gate keeps its design. The plan neither adopts E2 nor rules it out for the hermetic VRC family; plan §8 states the constraint (explicit snapshot save, modification time checked). Whether the VRC family wants it at all depends on T0.2: if onboarding is not a large share of a VRC run, the question dissolves, and if the Node tier retires the family, it never arises.

**C3. Node harness on "real" wallet code.** The branch plan specifies it as if feasible; my feasibility note found that the existing Node suites (`vrc-reference`, `credo-tsp-adapter`, `mediator-server`) do not import wallet modules and that headless operation of the wallet's TypeScript was never shown. Verified on 2026-10-22: `vrc-reference/package.json` has no `@bifold/core` dependency; `modules/vrc/services` and `modules/vrc/module` have no React Native imports, while `modules/vrc/vrc-manager.ts` and `vrc-hardware-signing.ts` do; 22 files in `modules/trust-tasks/module/` match the string `react-native` (which also matches `@credo-ts/react-native`). Resolution: the plan adopts the branch specification but marks the design conditional and puts a spike (T1.2) ahead of it.

- **Two plans, one sequence.** Keeping #252 as an adopted-by-reference sibling would have left the testing-speed sequence in two documents, one of them unmerged for three weeks, with the Node harness specified in one and its groundwork in the other.
- **Standing rationale needs to stay.** Principles 1 and 2, the simulated-biometrics rejection, the device rules and the other options in its §10 are constraints a future implementer would otherwise re-propose.
