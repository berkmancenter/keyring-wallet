## Part 3. Decisions

**R1. The spec separates a general standard from what we adopt.** Brendan: "I just want to distinguish what we propose for a QR code/link standard generally vs what we choose to adopt for ourselves. That should be clear in the documentation." Also: "if we are proposing a new auth flow that works well for us, we don't want to lock others in — our design should be modular."

- **The general standard** is what any wallet or inviter can adopt:
  - the link form and fields, reading, the host rules, size and rendering;
  - confirming before any network activity, verifying the DID, taking the transport only from the DID document;
  - handle security, and producer rules.

  It requires no particular protocol for the exchange.
- **Each flow states its first request** (spec section 5 rule 7, rewritten). The Trust Task first request is one defined option (new spec section 5a), holding the old rule 7 text unchanged. A flow may define a DIDComm, OpenID or other first request instead.
- **The two flows in the spec's section 4 table are labelled as adopted by Keyring and VTI.** They are examples of the format, not part of it.
- **Rule 4 (fresh key) now applies to requests the consumer signs.** It allows a flow to define a signature by an identifier the person chose at approval. Without that, a flow whose first request is signed by the person's chosen identifier, as SIOPv2's is, could never conform.
- **Rule 8 (no hint)** names discovery generally, with `trust-task-discovery` as the Trust Tasks case.
- **Plan section 1a** is a two-column table: general standard against what we adopt. It states the rule for documents: a choice of ours is stated as ours, never as a requirement of the link format.
- *Supersedes:* section 5 rule 7 as a requirement on every trigger (D12, and VTI-LNK-054 as drafted). Section 5a keeps its text for the flows that adopt it, so D12's reasoning about `parentThreadId` still holds there.

**R2. SIOPv2 is kept. The VTI specification does not deprecate it.** *Superseded later this session by R5 for Keyring and the Farm; its point about the VTI text stands.*
- "SIOPv2 is not a VTI flow" is acceptable as a statement of scope: VTI flows use Trust Tasks.
- Deprecating an established external protocol is not the link format's business, nor the VTI specification's.
- Keyring keeps SIOPv2 for relying parties outside VTI (plan K6).
- The Farm offers `sign-in` and keeps its SIOPv2 login. Whether and when it retires SIOPv2 is decided internally (R3).
- F2 gives a practical reason as well: wallets that claimed with the legacy QR can't use `sign-in` with the Farm until they hold its DID.
- If the Farm puts SIOPv2 behind a link, "Using another wallet?" describes it better than "Using an older wallet?". A SIOPv2 wallet is not necessarily older, only outside VTI.
- *Supersedes:* Glenn's proposal (alignment9) that SIOPv2 becomes the Farm's legacy login and is then removed, as a position Keyring endorses.
- *Not verified this session:* SIOPv2's current status at the OpenID Foundation. It was described from memory as an OpenID Foundation specification with deployments outside VTI.

**R3. The VTA Farm is run by our team.** Brendan: "the farm is under our teams control and we could align on that internally". The plan's dependency line no longer calls the Farm "not ours to schedule". Farm choices (the SIOPv2 window, adding the Farm's DID to the progress response, the code check after a claim, the DID host) are internal decisions, not negotiations. *Supersedes:* the plan's dependency statement, and the framing in `2026-10-08-bm.md` and `2026-10-09-bm.md` of the Farm as an external party whose reply is "treated as data".

**R4. A legacy-claim wallet gets to `sign-in` through the Farm's DID, decided internally.** *Made a requirement by R5.* Plan section 4 now records F2. The Farm adding its DID to the progress response is the fix being considered. It already plans to add mediator and DID-log hints there (plan section 7). Until it does, those wallets log in with SIOPv2.

## Part 8. No centralization as a principle, and the shorthand forms

**R7. "No centralization required" is a principle of its own** (plan section 1.1, principle 2). Brendan: "Another principle at the top should be no centralization required — anyone can host their own urls and flows."

- **What the principle says.** Taking part needs no registry, no shared link host and no namespace owner. Flow identifiers are whole URIs, so owners never collide. The shorthand forms work the same on every host.
- **The shared host's role, stated honestly.** It is a convenience, needed only because a phone camera opens an `https` link in an app whose build declares that host. A self-hosted link works through any wallet's in-app scanner, by pasting, and by camera in wallets that declare its host.
- **Sources.** This was implicit before: spec section 3 (any host meeting the host rules), the path form (P11), and VTI-LNK-090 to 092 (any link host's duties).
- **The spec's scope statement** now says it needs no central party.
- **Principle numbering.** The principles are now: modularity; no centralization; internal simplicity; keeping the two apart. References in Part 7 to principles 2 and 3 mean what are now 3 and 4.

**R8. The standard's shorthand forms, confirmed and listed** (plan section 1.2). Brendan asked to confirm that the standard supports optional ways to shorten the link, such as a `_type` that is only a path resolved against the link's own domain. Confirmed against the VTI draft (`trustoverip/dtgwg-vti-spec` PR #58, `spec/07a-links.md`) and our spec.

- **`_type` path form.** VTI-LNK-040 to 043; P11. It is mandatory for a producer when the flow is on the link's own host, and readers accept both forms.
- **Optional `_exp` and `_type`.** Spec section 2.
- **Raw colons and slashes.** Spec section 3; encoding rules per P15.
- **The 22-character handle floor.**
- **Agent names in `_from`.** Reserved, not yet admitted: VTI-LNK-032 says "a reader MUST refuse such a value with `unsupported-vid`, and a producer MUST NOT emit one" until agent names are defined.
- **The uppercase prefix.** Not in the standard; a later option pending a device test (Glenn's second note).

**Spec edit, bringing our text in line.** Our spec's section 2 still allowed only an absolute `_type`, although the path form was agreed (P11) and is in the VTI draft.
- Section 2's `_type` row now allows the path form.
- Section 4 gains a "Path form" paragraph matching VTI-LNK-040 to 043.
- Section 3's reading step 7 adds the resolution and `wrong-host`, which maps to `invalid` through the "every other reason" row.

**Still to bring in line.** The vectors have no path-form cases yet. The one non-URI `_type` vector (`bad-type-bare-word`, `_type=vta-claim`) is still correctly `bad-type`. These spec rules also still show their pre-agreement form, with the agreed state (`2026-10-09-bm.md` Part 8) governing until they are updated:
- `_from` (any VID up to 122 characters);
- `_id` (22 to 43);
- skew (60 s);
- the producer link cap (251 bytes, ASCII only).

Plan section 1.2 says so.
