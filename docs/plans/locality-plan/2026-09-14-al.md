## Findings

1. **A fresh wallet whose first mediation provisioning is interrupted could
   never initialize again.** `yarn e2e:vrc` failed twice at the emulator's agent
   init — *"Error during call to 'onInitializeContext' method in module
   'didcomm'"*, Retry included — while the simulator and the single-device smoke
   passed. Cause, in credo's `getMediationConnection`: the connection left behind
   when the didexchange request never reached the mediator (here, the emulator's
   outbound HTTPS hung) is found on every later launch, is not ready, and is
   waited on until the wait times out. Real devices never hit it because their
   first request always went through; any user on a bad network at first launch
   would. **Fix** (keyring-wallet #50, in the existing `@credo-ts/didcomm` yarn
   patch): wait 15 s, then delete the connection and its out-of-band record and
   receive the invitation again, once. **Reproduced deliberately**: fresh
   install, onboarding with the network up, the emulator's network cut the
   instant provisioning began (connection stranded in `request-sent`), relaunch
   with the network back — `never completed (state request-sent) — discarding it
   and receiving the mediator invitation again` → `Mediation granted` → Contacts.
   A guard test reads the patched module.

2. **Simulator suites green on that tree**: `e2e:smoke` and `e2e:vrc`
   (emulator + simulator). The witnessed and TSP simulator suites were not
   re-run this session.
