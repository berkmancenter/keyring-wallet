# 2026-09-13 — the iOS peripheral's first live runs, and what they took (al)

Companion to [`../locality-plan.md`](../locality-plan.md). Point-in-time; not
revised afterwards.

## What was run

`yarn e2e:vrc:witnessed:locality:devices` (keyring-wallet #40): a Galaxy A03s
(Android 13, low-end) and an iPhone 16 against a witness on this Mac using the
CoreBluetooth/noble sensor (keyring-bifold #49), policy `offered`. Twenty-three
attempts over the day; the last two passed from `main` with everything below
merged (wallet `a3433e9`, bifold `7dc1de2a`).

## What the day established

1. **The iOS peripheral works, and App Attest is fast enough.** The 2026-09-12
   companion's open question was whether `generateAssertion`, which cannot be
   pre-armed and therefore runs *inside* the witness's 400 ms RTT window, fits.
   Measured: `signingElapsedMs` = **77, 63, 72** on three runs. The witness
   matched the iPhone within 1 s of `Advertising started` once the scan bug (5)
   was fixed. Both legs confirmed (`ble-challenge-response/0.1`), both VWCs
   carried `localityConfirmed: true`, and both parties' witness-shares were
   verified and stored by the other.

2. **One OS prompt per exchange, on both platforms — a model change, decided
   today.** A witnessed exchange signs twice with the hardware key (VRC content,
   then the locality transcript a minute or so later) and each raised its own
   prompt. Alberto's call: minimum one, accepting the weaker statement. Android
   (both modules sign with the same StrongBox key): keys enrolled from now on
   carry a 300 s auth window (`setUserAuthenticationParameters`), both signers
   try `initSign` silently and prompt only on `UserNotAuthenticatedException`;
   keys enrolled earlier keep per-operation auth — and two prompts — until
   re-created. iOS (both prompts are `LAContext` policy gates over App Attest):
   a successful prompt is recorded `{at, mode, pid}` in-process and the other
   module honours it for 300 s. Evidence now attests user presence *within the
   window*, not per signature; `HARDWARE_ATTESTATION_FLOW.md` says so. Verified
   on both phones: `prompt opened a 300s auth window` → `Auth window still open
   … no second prompt` (Android); `Reusing user authentication from 2s ago`
   (iOS). keyring-bifold #51.

3. **The iOS pre-flight sheet is real (item 8), and it has to be.** The sheet was
   gated `Platform.OS === 'android'`. On iOS the first `CBPeripheralManager` —
   and CoreBluetooth's one-time system prompt — was therefore created at the
   radio phase; the prompt landed mid-exchange and the leg timed out
   (`localityMethod: none`). The sheet now shows on both platforms and its Allow
   requests `PERMISSIONS.IOS.BLUETOOTH`, which raises the prompt at
   witness-connect (`'Bluetooth'` handler enabled in the app's Podfile).
   keyring-bifold #50, keyring-wallet #45.

4. **A low-end Android phone exposed two Android bugs that are not about
   locality.** (a) Two independent foreground handlers (bifold core's
   `ActivityProvider`, the app's `useBCAgentSetup`) each restart mediator pickup,
   and credo's `initiateMessagePickup` stacks a fresh loop per call — after any
   OS permission dialog the wallet polled twice per second, and the A03s's
   inbound processing fell 52 s behind, so the mediator's keylist-update-response
   (sent in 40 ms) missed credo's 14 s connect timeout: every "URL not
   recognized" of the week. Fixed in the credo patch (`initiateMessagePickup`
   replaces the running loop) with a guard test. (b) `react-native-volume-manager`
   registers its lifecycle listener before assigning its fields; on a slow
   device the resumed activity's callback NPEs and kills the app at launch —
   patch-packaged. keyring-wallet #43. Also learned: the A03s cannot run the dev
   bundle at all for timing-sensitive DIDComm (release APK only), and even one
   1 s pickup loop is most of its JS thread (median lag 0.9 s, p90 9 s) — the
   poll interval on low-end devices is a plan question, not a bug.

5. **The macOS witness's scan needs duplicates on.** CoreBluetooth reports a
   peripheral once per scan when duplicates are off, and an iPhone is already
   being reported — for its continuity adverts — before the wallet puts our
   service UUID in its advertisement; the advert that matters was never
   delivered (the Galaxy, a fresh peripheral, matched in 2 s; the iPhone
   advertised 120 s unseen). `allowDuplicates: true`, with the existing
   `inFlight` set as the dedup. keyring-bifold #52.

6. **A fast radio leg exposed an ordering race in the witness-share.** The share
   is sent once, from "witness session complete", after waiting ≤ 30 s for the
   peer's discovery answer. With the iPhone's leg now done in 1 s, its VWC was
   stored 30 s before the Galaxy had even answered discovery; the share was
   skipped and the Galaxy's contact never showed a witness record. Wait is now
   ≤ 2 min (keyring-bifold #53). The right fix is to re-trigger the share from
   the discovery-answer consumer so no wait is involved — open.

7. **Harness.** The pre-flight sheet's timing follows the witness discovery round
   trip (seconds to over a minute), so every element lookup now clears it on a
   miss; locality runs get 300 s for the VRC contact and the ceremony markers;
   the iOS locality readout uses `idevicesyslog` when present, because Appium's
   `syslog` log on a real device evicts the peripheral's lines. keyring-wallet
   #44 and the housekeeping PR.
