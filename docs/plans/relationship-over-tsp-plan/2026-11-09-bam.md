## 2. Qualified: `_label`

The plan treated `_label` as an optional unverified display name. The thread limits it. It is for codes shown in person only; display text only, normalised, dropped and not truncated on a violation, no links, captioned as sender-written and unverified, not logged; the cap is on encoded bytes (64 code points percent-encoded can reach about 768 bytes). It is **provisional**: it needs the working group, and the fallback is the name in `propose.reason`, confirmed on the acceptance. WP1, WP3 and §9 Q8 now say so.

## 3. Reconciled: open decision 3 (which key signs)

The plan's decision 3 asked for the issuer VID or the relationship DID. For `shared-connect` the thread answers it: the issuer VID, under `authentication`, one VID for issuer, signer and sender. The relationship credential inside `issue` stays signed by the relationship key. What stays with Alberto is Keyring's connection-based path (the relationship DID signs there today, `ceremony.ts:1405`) and the interim of accepting either key inbound. Decision 3 in the plan is reworded to that remainder.
