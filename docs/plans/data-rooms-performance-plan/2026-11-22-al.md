## Findings and decisions

### 1. The brainstorm, verbatim

- **Concluded:** recorded as the plan's requirements (plan §3). The list, as given on 2026-11-22:

  > Data rooms
  > Keyring startup
  > Keyring -> VTA connect
  > VTA -> VTC
  > VTA -> Data room
  > No caching yet
  > Optimistic
  > Encrypted
  > Removed when leave data room or VTC
  > Lazy loading of content
  > Websockets
  > Identities??
  > Multiple identities - need to manage and load them intelligently
  > Learn from Signal and Matrix - use best practices, with our VTI philosophy
  > You can view VTC and data room content before you're even connected from cached content
  > In the background, things are refreshed and updated in a priority order, of what is most likely or recent or important.
  > Also consider parallel loading
  > (e.g. adding new messages before seeing any messages deletion)
  > Role of the mediator and its influence

  And the two goals: "Some of the early goals would be to have something so it's like the Signal Keyring feedback group and work similar to a group chat but within a data room/VTC"; "early ideas is to prove that context moves with you, like between VTCs, like a colleague and something else".
- **Evidence:** the owner's message of 2026-11-22, with its spelling normalised and nothing added or removed.
- **Supersedes:** nothing; this is the first record.
- **Plan sections changed:** plan §3 (created).
- **Test impact:** none yet.

### 2. The readings are the recorder's

- **Concluded:** each requirement in plan §3 carries a one-line reading of what it asks for. Those readings were written by the recorder, not by the owner, and are marked so in the plan. Two of them interpret more than the words say and are flagged for the owner: "No caching yet" is read as "beyond what exists, nothing is cached" rather than "nothing is cached at all", because the message cache, kept listings and rows-at-start are on main at the baseline (plan §2); and "view content before you're even connected" is read as going further than today's cache, which shows only until the first listing and comes down if it fails, so it is plan §11 Q1 rather than a requirement taken as read.
- **Evidence:** plan §2 (the constants and files at `origin/main` `7856855ed`).
- **Supersedes:** nothing.
- **Plan sections changed:** plan §3, §11.
- **Test impact:** none.
