# 2026-11-27 — "Answers other people" on an HCP agent: Off is final in P1

Companion to [`../agent-connect-plan.md`](../agent-connect-plan.md). Not revised afterwards.

## Summary
<!-- plan-part: human -->

> **Human part: for people deciding and reviewing.** What was concluded and what it
> asks for. The findings below are authoritative; if the two disagree, the findings win.

On an agent connected through HCP, "Answers other people" is an HCP directive, which the person's VTA delivers to
the agent with everything it reads. Turning it on sets a directive that permits answering, and turning it off sets
one that denies it. HCP makes a deny win over a permit, and the task that removes a directive comes only in P2. So
in P1, once the person turns it off for a connection, it stays off: a later On would lose to the Off.

The plan's §6 says turning "Answers other people" off needs no confirmation. On an HCP agent it now asks first,
because the step cannot be undone in P1, and afterwards the switch shows Off and cannot be changed. The base path is
unchanged. In P2, removing a directive lifts the restriction, and the confirmation goes away with it.

**This was decided in the backlog brief (`f207d5a60`, part 4) and approved by the plan owner by relay on
2026-11-27; his own confirmation is needed before `main`.** Pull request #1106 builds it. This companion records the
decision and proposes the six new strings in English, French and Brazilian Portuguese.

### 1. DECISION, §6 "HCP lane screens" and §7.29: on an HCP agent, Off is final for the connection in P1

- **The constraint.** HCP 6.4, the bullet "Directives only narrow" (`specification.md:1743`): "Where two directives
  that apply disagree, `deny` prevails over `ask-first`, and `ask-first` over `permit`." (The companion
  `2026-11-25-bam.md` cites other lines of the same section: `:1721-1722` are the directive template's `action` and
  `effect`, `:1733` the members table, `:1745` the bullet on clients that claim to follow directives.) The
  precedence has three effects; this decision uses two. A future "ask each time" setting would be an `ask-first`
  directive, which a `deny` also overrides, so the same restriction would hold for it until
  `hcp/directives/remove`. A directive is set with `hcp/directives/set` 0.1; at the suite
  commit, removing one is `hcp/directives/remove`, which the suite specifies (`specs/hcp/directives/` holds `list`,
  `remove` and `set`) and HCP provides for (6.4, `:1742`: the Context Subject "sets and removes directives through a
  Management Client"). The companion `2026-11-25-bam.md` finding 11 places `set` in P1 and `directives/{list,remove}`
  in P2 (V2 = H2). **So Off-is-final is a scope decision of this plan, not a gap in HCP or the suite**: lifting it is
  this plan's to schedule, not something to wait for upstream. No pinned implementation serves any `hcp/*` task yet,
  `set` included.
- **What follows.** On sets a `permit` directive; Off sets a `deny` directive. After an Off, a later On would set a
  `permit` that the `deny` overrides, so the agent would still not answer: a switch that showed On would be wrong.
  The connection's setting is therefore final once it is Off, until Keyring sends `hcp/directives/remove`, which is
  not in P1.
- **Decided:** three states. Off with no directive ever set (the agent's default is to answer only its owner, §3.13,
  and no directive permits more), On (a `permit` was set), and Off for good (a `deny` was set:
  the switch shows Off and is disabled). Turning it off asks first, with the strings in finding 2.
- **This departs from §6's base-path rule** ("Turning it off needs no confirmation"). The base path keeps that rule;
  the exception is on an HCP agent only, and only while `hcp/directives/remove` is not in Keyring's scope.
- **Lifted in P2:** with `hcp/directives/remove`, turning it off removes the `permit` instead of setting a `deny`, a
  later On sets a new `permit`, and the confirmation goes away.
- **Rejected:** offering On again after Off (it would show a setting that does not hold), and setting On and Off as
  two directives without the confirmation (the person would not learn the step is final until it was too late).
- **Status:** decided in the backlog brief at `f207d5a60`, approved by the plan owner by relay on 2026-11-27. His
  own confirmation is needed before this reaches `main`.
- **Changes:** §6 "HCP lane screens" gains the bullet "Answers other people on an HCP agent"; §7.29's C5 names the
  restriction.

### 2. PROPOSED, the six strings

These strings are new and proposed in all three languages; the French and Portuguese need a native reader before
they reach the localization files. The en column is the text §6 now quotes. They are the strings #1106 uses
(`hcp/answersOn/copy.ts`), where they are held to this table.

| Where | en | fr | pt-br |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confirmation, title | Turn off answers for "<name>"? You can't turn them back on for this connection yet. | Désactiver les réponses de « <name> » ? Vous ne pourrez pas encore les réactiver pour cette connexion. | Desativar as respostas de "<name>"? Você ainda não poderá reativá-las para esta conexão. |
| Confirmation, button | Turn off | Désactiver | Desativar |
| Confirmation, button | Keep it on | Laisser activé | Manter ativado |
| The disabled switch's line | Off for this connection. Connect it again to change this. | Désactivé pour cette connexion. Connectez-le de nouveau pour changer cela. | Desativado para esta conexão. Conecte-o outra vez para mudar isso. |
| Directive text, On | You may answer other people in rooms you've been added to. | Vous pouvez répondre aux autres personnes dans les salons où vous avez été ajouté. | Você pode responder a outras pessoas nas salas em que foi adicionado. |
| Directive text, Off | Don't answer other people. | Ne répondez pas aux autres personnes. | Não responda a outras pessoas. |

The directive's text is the person's words about the directive, which HCP shows them when it is set (HCP 6.4,
`text`), so it is written to the agent in the second person.
