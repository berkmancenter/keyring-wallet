## Summary

The product owner read the plan on 2026-11-22, after a meeting with its author, and accepted it as the **P0 of a wider vision** the lab has been researching: a person's AI agent that carries its memory, its permissions and its record of what it did with the person, across models, across programs and across communities. The plan builds the first rung of that: one person, their own Claude Code session, one QR pairing, a chat, rooms, a revoke. What the wider research adds, and where it differs from this plan, is finding 1; none of it changes the plan.

Four things were decided or confirmed. **Mentions are a demo requirement.** The plan's text handle (`@<handle>`) stays for P0, and the upstream gap behind it (no `mentions` record member, no mention-scoped delivery) is raised now rather than left "not yet raised": it is VTI-Q26 in [`../../VTI_UPSTREAM_FINDINGS.md`](../../VTI_UPSTREAM_FINDINGS.md), with the other gaps the plan depends on as VTI-Q27 to VTI-Q36 (finding 7). **The plan is implemented in the one-codebase repository**: `bifold/packages/*` is part of this repository since #970, so a step is one PR here, and the plan's sentence about "paired bifold PRs" is stale (finding 3). **Phase 0 and per-step tests before implementing are wanted** as the plan already states them (finding 4). **This work is the top priority for a demo**, and the findings work adjusts to it (finding 6).

Later the same day the product owner added two positions and said the lab may edit the plan itself rather than wait for its author. So the plan is edited here: the stale §7 sentence is replaced and the ledger's header maps its entries to the findings file (findings 3, 7, 12). **Every step runs on the lab first, in full, before the VTA Farm or anyone else's community, and every gap met is recorded in the findings file in that step's PR** (plan D25, §7, §8.2; finding 10). **The My Agent tab is proposed to show the AI agents**, with the VTA's own screens moving under Settings or the menu; it is recorded as a conditional design (plan D26, §6) because two things must be decided first: what the VTA is called once "My Agent" means the AI agent, and where the rooms screens sit (plan §11 Q2; finding 11). Until then the plan's first layout, the AI agents as a card on the My Agent tab, is what gets built, and the mockups hold. It asks the product owner and the plan's author for Q2's two answers, and nobody else for anything.

## Findings and decisions

### 1. The plan is the P0 of a wider vision; what the vision adds is not in this plan

- **Concluded:** the lab's AI research (two rounds, 2026-11-19 to 2026-11-20: a landscape of person-controlled AI, and a brainstorm on models, memory, community rules and the person's screens) describes the whole; this plan is its first buildable piece and is accepted as such. The research is a set of pages and notes, not a plan, and is not linked here because it is not in this repository.
- **What the research covers that the plan does not, by design:** switching the model behind an agent, and what is lost on a switch; where the agent's memory lives and how it moves between programs (the plan uses the VTA's per-context memory and does not read it, plan §3.7); a community's AI rules shown as a card before anything is shared (the plan has a yes/no setting, advisory, plan §3.8); labels for what is proved, seen or only said about an agent, its program and its model; "acting for" another person; a person's own standing terms (MyTerms, P1 in plan §1.1); a hosted or headless agent (P1, plan §3.12). The research's screens (21 frames) drew a connect flow by scanning a code and comparing a short number on both screens; the plan types a four-digit number from the terminal into Keyring and never shows it in Keyring (plan §3.2, D8). The plan's form is kept: it is the decided design, with its reasoning in the author's companion (finding 10 there).
- **Where the two agree:** the agent is a separate program, never the person's VTA, and the screens keep "your agent" for the VTA and "AI agent" for the session (plan §1.4, D20); the agent's authority is the ACL entry and its record of ownership is a delegation credential (plan §3.4); revoke is one action and never promised instant (plan §3.9); the "AI agent" mark on anything the agent writes (plan §3.6); a fixed, model-free reply to strangers (plan §3.13).
- **Framing rule carried over:** Keyring is a research project of the lab, not a product of any state or community; a plan or page describes it so. The plan already does.
- **Supersedes:** nothing in the plan. It supersedes the research's connect-flow mock (compare a number) as a proposal for P0.
- **Plan sections changed:** none.
- **Test impact:** none.

The product owner's words: "we should if there is a gap upstream add it". So the gap is raised as a question now, instead of waiting for the ledger's promotion rules: VTI-Q26 asks for a registered `mentions` record member and for delivery or a read leaf scoped to the records that mention the presenter.

- **Supersedes:** the ledger's "not yet raised" for AC-04 (the ledger file itself is not edited here; finding 7).
