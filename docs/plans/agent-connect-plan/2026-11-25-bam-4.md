## Findings and decisions

### 1. AC-36 is filed (Brendan, 2026-11-25)

The draft written on the local branch `spec/auth-oob-device-enrol` was reviewed twice and filed, on Brendan's yes, as the draft #1386. The ledger's "recorded, not filed" and the plan's "a draft PR only on Brendan's yes" are replaced by the link.

### 2. What the filed draft settles

- **Administrator.** The signer of `grant` must be a DID that could write the same entry by `acl/grant` 0.3. This makes precise §3.2's "an administrator of that VTA".
- **`keys: all`.** A `keys` scope of `all` is refused for `deviceEnrol`.
- **Four digits.** The four-digit `MatchNumber` is ask A12 of the draft (plan §11 Q8, D8); unchanged.

### 3. Contacts deferred; §11 Q7(a) stays open

An earlier version of the draft carried `contacts` (`ownerDid`, `chatDid`, `mediatorDid`) in the `redeem` result. Brendan approved cutting it to a follow-up, because `redeem`'s response is not signed by the VTA, so the agent could not authenticate those DIDs from it. The `redeem` route for §11 Q7(a) therefore needs a VTA-signed `redeem` first. Q7 stays open with no recommendation.

### 4. Plan sections changed

§3.2 (the conditional paragraph: the administrator definition, the `keys` rule, the link to #1386 and the deferral), §10 (the asks table's AC-36 row), §11 Q7 (the deferral noted in its recommendation cell), and the review index (row 10). Ledger: the not-registered list's AC-36 entry and AC-36's Severity.
