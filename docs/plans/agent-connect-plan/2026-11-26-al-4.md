### 10. PROPOSAL, §7.7: which agents are contacts

- **Old (7.7):** "one row per agent not `pairing` or `notFinished`". That includes expired and revoked agents.
- **Proposed (7.7):** one row per agent whose status is "Connected", "Connected until", "Not connected right now" or "Revoke not confirmed". An "Expired", "Revoked" or "Not finished" agent, and one still pairing, is listed only under "AI agents".

**Option A, only the agents that still have, or may still have, access (recommended).**
- **Pros:**
  - Contacts holds the agents a person can deal with, and "Revoke not confirmed" stays in view because it may still have access.
  - An ended agent does not sit among people.
- **Cons:** an expired agent leaves Contacts the moment it expires.

**Option B, every agent except pairing and "Not finished" (the old line).**
- **Pros:** no change, and every agent is one tap away.
- **Cons:** revoked agents stay among people indefinitely, with nothing to do there.

**Recommendation: A.**

### 11. PROPOSAL, §7.7: the driver named, and how the rotation is learned

- **Proposed (7.7):** "**The driver** (`connected-agents/`) wires the pairing machine of §7.2 to the carriage of §7.5 with the real ports:
  - the VTA's `TrustTaskHTTPS` endpoint (as sign-in posts);
  - the agent's KMS for the administrator's signature;
  - `verifyDocumentProof` for the VTA's answers;
  - `VtaClient` for the access list.

  The lockout and the spent request ids persist in the app's storage. While a pairing waits for the agent's registration and rotation, Keyring re-lists the entry on a timer until it is confirmed or the pairing is "Not finished" (§11 Q7(c): re-list)."
- **Why:** 7.7's line named no driver, and §11 Q7(c) leaves open how Keyring learns of the rotation. Q7 already says "(c) and (d) do not block P0, which can re-list". The machine's `checkEnrolment` and `NOT_FINISHED_AFTER_MS` (10 minutes) are built for exactly that.

**Option A, re-list on a timer while the pairing waits (recommended).**
- **Pros:** it needs nothing new from VTI, and the machine already supports it.
- **Cons:** it makes several listings during the wait, at most 10 minutes per pairing.

**Option B, wait for the VTA's notice or the chat's first message.**
- **Pros:** fewer calls.
- **Cons:** neither exists in P0.

**Recommendation: A, revisited when Q7(c) is answered.**

### 12. PROPOSAL, §6 and §7.7: an expired agent is offered "Connect again" and "Remove", not "Extend"

- **Old (§6):** "Extend" for a temporary agent, and the canvas drew it on an "Expired" row too.
- **Measured** (the test lane, VTA 0.84.0, one scratch run):
  - right after expiry the entry is still listed, `acl/update` 0.3 with a new `expiresAt` is accepted, and the agent signs in again;
  - once the VTA's sweeper has run, `acl/update` answers `acl/update:notFound`, and only a new `acl/grant` brings the key back.
- **The sweeper, at `97fda9813`:** `sweep_expired` deletes expired entries (`vta-sweepers/src/acl_sweeper.rs:41`). It runs from the VTA's periodic task (`vta-service/src/server.rs:2167`) every `auth.session_cleanup_interval` seconds, default 600 (`vti-common/src/config.rs:756`), set by the operator.
- **So** an "Expired" row is either still there or gone, and Keyring cannot tell without asking.
- **Unmeasured:** whether the device registration and `deviceId` survive the sweep (is a re-grant the same agent?), and a revoke of an entry already gone.

**Option A, "Connect again" (a new pairing with the name filled in, which replaces the record when it connects) and "Remove"; "Extend" only before expiry (recommended).**
- **Pros:** one behaviour whatever the sweeper has done, and access never comes back without the terminal's number.
- **Cons:** a new pairing even a minute after expiry.

**Option B, "Extend" on an expired row, falling back on `notFound` to a new `acl/grant` of the old key behind Approve and the biometric.**
- **Pros:** one tap.
- **Cons:** access comes back without a fresh pairing, and whether that is the same agent is unmeasured.

**Recommendation: A.** "Remove" on an expired row revokes the entry if the VTA still lists it, and drops the record either way.

### 15. PROPOSAL, §7.7: Revoke reaches no one late

- **Proposed (7.7):** "**Before any build turns the developer flag on,** Revoke (step 7.10) is in it: until then the Revoke actions are not shown, and a build with the flag on and without step 7.10 is not released to anyone."
- **Why:** hiding Revoke until 7.10 is acceptable only if no one can connect an agent before they can revoke it. A connected agent that cannot be revoked from the app must not reach anyone, flag or not. The 4-hour grant limits the risk but does not remove it.

**Option A, hidden until 7.10, with this as a release condition (recommended).**
- **Pros:** 7.7 lands without waiting on 7.10.
- **Cons:** it is a condition to watch at release, not a test.

**Option B, build the Revoke sheet in 7.7 and wire it in 7.10.**
- **Pros:** the screen is ready early.
- **Cons:** a sheet that does nothing would be in the code.

**Recommendation: A.**

### 18. PROPOSAL, §7.7: which persona issues the agent's VDC

- **The gap:**
  - §3.4 says the VDC's issuer is "the persona the owner is acting as" and that the multi-profile plan governs which.
  - That plan has not decided it, and the app has no default persona. Personas are per community (`VtiIdentityStore.listPersonas()`, each with its `communityDid`).
  - The connect must still name one. It is the agent's `ownerPersonaDid`, shown as "Belongs to <owner>".
- **The multi-profile plan** is [`editable-multi-profile-plan.md`](../editable-multi-profile-plan.md).
- **Proposed (7.7):** with exactly one persona, that one. With more than one, the connect asks which, in one row "Acting as" above the memories. This holds until the multi-profile plan decides it.

**Option A, the only persona, or ask when there are several (recommended).**
- **Pros:** no guess is made for the owner. The common case, one persona, adds no step.
- **Cons:** one more row, and its words ("Acting as") are new. With several personas the owner meets a choice the profile plan may later make differently.

**Option B, the persona of the community used most recently.**
- **Pros:** no new row.
- **Cons:** it is a guess. "Belongs to <owner>" could show a persona the owner did not mean, to every room the agent joins.

**Option C, wait for the multi-profile plan.**
- **Pros:** one decision, made once.
- **Cons:** 7.7 cannot finish its connect until then.

**Recommendation: A.** The canvas has no "Acting as" row yet; the UI/UX lane draws it if this is accepted.
