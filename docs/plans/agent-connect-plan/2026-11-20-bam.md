### 11. The VDC is the owner's record, not a room credential

- **Concluded:** the VDC is what the owner issues when delegating to an agent they answer for. It is not presented in room operations (rooms use the agent's own VAC leaf). ### 20. The open questions, resolved with the owner

- **Concluded (the owner accepted the recommendations):**
  - **Number length: four digits, not two** (was Q14; plan §3.2, D8, mockups 03 and 04). Reasoning: the community's two digits make a blind guess 1 in 100, and under the lockout (three wrong in 60 minutes, then a 15-minute lock renewed by each further wrong number) an attacker gets at most six guesses in any hour, so 6% an hour, over 50% in a day of persistence. Four digits make it 1 in 10,000: at most 6 in 10,000 (0.06%) an hour and under 1% a day, for two more keystrokes. Derivation changed to the first four bytes of the hash as a big-endian integer, mod 10,000, zero-padded; the bias (2^32 mod 10,000 = 7,296) is below one part in 400,000. The first lockout rule left the post-lock behaviour unstated; it now says a wrong number made while three are still inside the window renews the lock, which is what the six-an-hour bound needs. The wrong-number mockup shows the typed four digits under the notice, a small addition to the copy (plan §6).
  - **Lockout and reply limits kept as set** (was Q15): 3 wrong in 60 minutes with a 15-minute lock, a 5-minute code, one fixed reply per author per room per 60 minutes and 20 an hour. They are Keyring's own, with no VTI precedent beyond the PIN lock, so they are cheap to change (D8, D14).
  - **fr and pt-br review is a release gate** (was Q16; plan §3.13, D16, step 10 acceptance, the overview test table): a native reader of each language approves the fixed-reply wording before release, recorded in the plugin repository's release checklist, with a release step that fails without the record.
