## Findings and decisions

### 1. A decision owner is not a push authority (applied to S6)

- **Before:** "force-pushing only those two feature branches after Brendan agrees to it", and "Owner of the decision to drop one instead: Brendan". Read alone, either sentence lets an implementer force-push or delete on Brendan's agreement.
- **Rule it meets:** Alberto's standing instructions for Keyring sessions name force-pushes, and any branch delete that is not a `test/*` branch or a merged PR's own head, as actions that need his own word every time. A relay does not count.
- **Now:** Brendan agrees to the rebase and owns the decision to drop a branch. The force-push or delete that carries either out also needs Alberto's own word, given to the session that runs it.
- **Changed:** S6 only (its step sentence and its "Done when" line), and this plan's §11 row.
