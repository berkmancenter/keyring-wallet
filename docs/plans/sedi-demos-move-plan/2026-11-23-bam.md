The earlier SEDI modularity note ([`/srv/dev/scratch/2026-11-23-bam-sedi-modularity.md`](/srv/dev/scratch/2026-11-23-bam-sedi-modularity.md), a scratch file, not in the repository) was written against pre-fold commits and recommended, for removal, moving `WebsDidResolver`'s dependency into `utils/`. Re-checked against the folded `main`, the dependency is on a smaller and cleaner set than a folder move would suggest: the CESR primitives, the KEL verifier, four `webs/` files and one `identity/` file are kept in place, and the real obstacle is a demo flag gating the verifying resolver. That is the plan's D1 and S1. The note's counts held to within a few files; its paths needed the `modules/` prefix and `C/modules/trust-tasks/module/` for the flag files.

## Findings

### 1. The keep set is five areas, not one file

- **Concluded:** keep `cesr/*`, `guardian/kel.ts`, `webs/{identifier,document,resolve,rootKel}.ts`, `identity/keriEvents.ts`; keep `guardian/chain.ts` and `holder/controller.ts` after decoupling (plan §5, D2). The demo is everything else under `modules/sedi/`.
- **Evidence:** `utils/WebsDidResolver.ts:3-4` is the only non-demo runtime import of `modules/sedi`; `triggerLink.ts` and `oobSignIn.ts` do not import it. `guardian/kel.ts` is imported by `webs/resolve.ts`, `identity/keriEvents.ts` and `holder/controller.ts`, and imports only `cesr` and noble. `webs/README.md` states there is no second KEL verifier.
- **Supersedes:** the note's option 3 step "relocate `WebsDidResolver`'s dependency into `utils/`". Moving files would break `kel.ts`'s importers and duplicate the verifier; leaving them in place and deleting around them does neither.

### 2. The verifying resolver is gated by a demo flag

- **Concluded:** remove the gate (D1).
- **Evidence:** `modules/sedi/webs/resolve.ts:15` imports `isStateVerifiedApproversEnabled` and `:146` refuses with `switchedOff`. `getArtifact` is ungated, which is why community sign-in works today without the flag. The note did not mention this gate.
- **Supersedes:** the note's statement that removal needs only the dependency relocated.

### 3. The umbrella kill is shared with two non-SEDI features

- **Concluded:** delete the clause from `trustedApproversFlag.ts` and `confirmItsYouFlag.ts`; their own `*_REMOTE_OFF` switches stay (plan §2.4, R5).
- **Evidence:** `SEDI_KERI_REMOTE_OFF` is read at `trustedApproversFlag.ts:31`, `stateVerifiedApproversFlag.ts:25`, `confirmItsYouFlag.ts:40`, `holder/stateCardFlag.ts:29` and `developerTestState.ts:78`; `module/sediKeriRemoteOff.ts:3-4` records the 2026-11-10 decision (Alberto) of one switch per feature plus one umbrella kill. This plan removes the umbrella; the feature switches are what that decision already gave each feature.

### 4. Paths differ from the note

- **Evidence:** the trust-tasks module is `bifold/packages/core/src/modules/trust-tasks/` (the note's `trust-tasks/…` is relative to `modules/`); the flag files are in its `module/` folder. `vtiLinks.ts` claims `keyring://guardian-card` at `:124` and throws `GUARDIAN_CARD_LINK_OFF` at `:705`; `ceremony.ts` registers the guardian-evidence handler through a lazy `require`; `ApproverOfferModal.tsx` imports `guardianEvidence` statically, which is the hardest call site to cut. `e2e/lib/testids.allow.json` also exists beside `testids.json` and `testids.app.json`.

### 5. Test classification needs a read, not a rename

- **Evidence:** `modules/sedi/__tests__/` mixes kept and demo tests (list in plan S4); `websResolver.test.ts` imports `../guardian` and `WebsDidResolver.test.ts` imports types from `modules/sedi/webs/resolve`; `guardianK0.test.ts` tests the chain with SEDI schemas. The `k0-utah-v2` fixtures are cited by path in `keri-holder-plan/2026-11-16-al.md` (`:66`, `:172`), which is why they stay.

### 6. Unmerged SEDI branches

- **Evidence:** `origin/feat/sedi-k0-developer-rows` (3 ahead, 53 behind) and `origin/feat/sedi-state-office` (15 ahead, 43 behind, adds `tools/sedi-state-office/`). The note's six old `keyring-bifold` branches are not remotes of this repository. Positions on them: plan D3.
