## What §2.4 now says, and where each part comes from

- **The member is a VTA persona, not the phone.** F19, from upstream's own
  join guide.
- **Keyring runs the ceremony as the persona, with borrowed keys.** F20 closed
  the question the branch's §2.4 still left open (does the VTA seal for the
  persona, or does the client?). openvtc keeps a persona's keys as
  references, fetches the secret with `keys/export-secret/0.1`, and signs and
  seals locally; its `join_lifecycle_e2e.rs` says submission *"never touches
  the VTA"*. Keyring's shipped code does the same: keyring-bifold `c6d8f768`
  (build 220), `VtaClient.ensurePersona` borrows the key-agreement **and**
  signing keys into the wallet's KMS and records the persona with their KMS
  ids. The branch's line *"its keys never leave the VTA"* was written before
  F20, and is not carried over.
- **The card arrives inline in `allow`, and Keyring keeps it**
  (`VtiCommunityStore`, `verdict.with.vmc`). The branch's §2.4 said *"the VTA
  receives and holds the card"*. That is not what is built, so §2.4 states
  what is.

## What this leaves open, for Alberto

1. **Where the card lives.** §2.6's rule is *no local mirror of state a
   counterparty owns*. Under B the card is the persona's, and upstream's
   design has the VTA's vault hold it. Keyring keeps its own copy from the
   inline `allow`. Either §2.6 names the card as something Keyring holds (it
   is a credential, and it verifies with no agent reachable), or the card
   moves to the VTA and Keyring renders it from there. Not decided here.
2. **Borrowed keys are kept.** The borrowed persona keys stay in the
   wallet's KMS across restarts; F20 said *"for the session, never persisted
   elsewhere"*. That is a custody question for §3.2 of the parent, and it is
   noted here, not changed.
