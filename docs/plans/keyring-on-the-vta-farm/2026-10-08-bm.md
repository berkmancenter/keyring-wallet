## F1 — The proposal, as received

The Farm builds VTA slots ahead of demand and parks them: a name, a published identity and storage, with nothing running and no admin. An admin or partner issues a **claim link** with a use counter. Opening it reserves a slot and shows a one-time QR. Keyring scans the QR and sends a temporary admin key; the Farm records it as super admin and starts the VTA. The VTA answers on its own hostname; Keyring connects, swaps the temporary key for a long-lived admin key, becomes the default manager, then registers a separate durable login identity with the Farm (the user's Farm credential). No secret is shared in advance.

Stated trade-offs: names are fixed at build (for example `brave-otter`); whoever scans owns the slot; losing the phone loses the account, deliberately; stock is cheap but not free. Stated timing: about 8 minutes cold against about 2 minutes from the pool.

## F2 — Most of the phone's half already exists

Checked in `bifold/packages/core/src/modules/trust-tasks/module/`:

- `agentHostConnection.ts` implements a scan-to-connect flow with the same shape. The QR holds exactly `{ vta_did, callback_url }`; `parseAgentHostQr` returns undefined for anything else (it requires exactly those two keys). The callback must be HTTPS on an allow-list (`AGENT_HOST_SITES = ['ic3.dev', 'firstperson.dev']`); the QR lives `AGENT_HOST_QR_LIFETIME_MS = 5 * 60 * 1000`. The phone POSTs `{ admin_did }` (its own Ed25519 did:key), polls through `provisioning` to `awaiting_mobile`, links, rotates, and POSTs `{ status: "connected" }`.
- `vtaAgent.ts` `finishLink` is the single link-completion path (called from the agent-host, enrolment and device paths), and `VtaClient.rotateManagerKey` (`acl/swap-key/0.1`, `VtaClient.ts`) swaps the linking key for a long-lived one. Swap-loss recovery is handled in the same module.
- `vtiLinks.ts` is the one entry point for scan, paste and deep link.
- `VtiIdentityStore.ts` keeps one manager key per VTA in the Credo KMS.

So the proposal's "scan, send temporary key, swap for a long-lived key" is the existing agent-host flow. The proposal is mainly a **Farm-side** feature that a Keyring release already half-speaks.

It also overlaps a request already made. [`own_agent_subtask.md`](./own_agent_subtask.md) §5 asks the Farm for U1, a QR of Keyring's `keyring://vta/enrol` offer, so the phone hands its key to the Farm itself. Two QR payloads now exist for the same job (the enrolment offer and the agent-host `{vta_did, callback_url}`); the Farm must be told which one it is being asked to build, or it will build a third.

## F3 — What Keyring does not have

1. **A Farm identity / credential registration step.** The agent-host flow ends at `connected`. Nothing registers a durable login identity with the Farm.
2. **A "default manager" concept.** There is a per-VTA manager key and an active agent, nothing called default manager. Decide whether it is a new notion or the active agent under another name.
3. **An ownership signal for super admin.** [`pnm_cnm_subtask.md`](../openvtc-integration-plan/pnm_cnm_subtask.md) §4.7 (lines 754-782) states that super-admin is a session-claim tier and that *"ownership is not on the wire"*. A claim-link flow that makes the scanner super admin does not give the phone a way to know it owns the VTA; the local declaration that section proposes would have to be written at claim time.
4. **Hardware-backed custody of the admin key.** The key is a Credo KMS did:key today. The proposal's "whoever scans owns it" makes that key the whole account.
5. **Recovery reconciled with "no recovery".** The proposal makes loss of the phone final. Keyring ships `LostPhoneCard`, `NewPhoneOffer`, `VtaNewPhoneOffer` and `KeysMovedNotice` (under `trust-tasks/screens/`), so the app currently promises a way back. Either the claim flow suppresses those screens or the "no recovery" stance is dropped. `own_agent_subtask.md` §4 (backup owner) and U3 are the existing positions. I could not confirm whether the VTA records the scanner's `admin_did` at mint: `docs/VTI_UPSTREAM_FINDINGS.md` VTI-11 (line 512) records an empty ACL for a fresh **VTC**'s `admin_did`, which is a different component, so it is a pointer to check and not evidence.

Farm-side work (not this repository, unverified): the slot pool and replenisher, the claim-link and reservation service, a record-key-as-super-admin and start endpoint, and the use counter and issuer controls.

## F4 — Gaps in the proposal itself

- **Payload shape.** In the existing parser the callback URL is the secret and an extra key makes `parseAgentHostQr` reject the QR. Any new field (slot name, expiry, claim id) needs a parser and format change, not only a Farm change.
- **Callback allow-list.** A partner-issued claim link on a partner domain is rejected by the HTTPS allow-list. The allow-list is a security control; widening it needs its own decision.
- **"Answers on its hostname".** Keyring reaches a VTA by DID plus mediator, and `did:webvh` resolution is not yet available in Credo (parent plan §3.4, §4). A hostname-only reachability story does not fit.
- **Claimed but never connected.** The temporary admin grant is reported to last about an hour (not re-verified by me). A slot that is claimed and never finished needs expiry and reclaim, or the pool leaks.
- **Claim-link security.** Add a short expiry and revocation; a use counter alone leaves a leaked link valid until the count runs out.
- **Fixed names.** `brave-otter` names are chosen at build and cannot be chosen by the user. Whether that is acceptable for a did:webvh identity that is hard to rename is a product question.

## F5 — Position

Worth pursuing as a **Farm-side** feature that reuses Keyring's existing flow. The wallet work is small to moderate (Farm credential registration, default-manager decision, QR/allow-list changes, writing the ownership declaration). Custody and recovery are a separate, larger piece and must be decided before the "no recovery" stance ships. The Farm service is the main effort and is not ours to schedule. Under the plan's dependency rule this blocks nothing and is not a phase.

Alternative rejected for now: a pre-shared secret or account-first claim. The proposal avoids it deliberately, and `own_agent_subtask.md` U1 already shows the account-first path costs a copy-and-paste round trip.

## Positions agreed by bm (2026-10-08)

Brendan accepted the recommendations below as **Keyring's position going into talks with the Farm owners**. The Farm has not adopted any of them, and the parent plan does not change. They answer the open questions above and extend F4 and F5.

1. **QR shape.** The QR keeps `{vta_did, callback_url}`. The callback is one-time, and the Farm enforces expiry and single use server-side. A field is added only when the wallet needs to see it, and the format is versioned. The conflict with [`own_agent_subtask.md`](./own_agent_subtask.md) U1 (the `keyring://vta/enrol` payload) is resolved first, so the Farm builds one payload and not two.
2. **Callback allow-list.** The list holds Farm domains only. Partners issue links that land on a Farm callback. If the list is ever opened, the wallet reads a signed, fetched config.
3. **Super-admin ownership.** The Farm or VTA gives an explicit ownership signal. This is the highest-priority question for the Farm owners.
4. **Unfinished claims.** The scan window is about 5 minutes and scan-to-connect is about 1 hour. After that the Farm destroys and rebuilds the slot. A slot that has had an admin never returns to the pool.
5. **Claim links.** A link carries a use counter, a short issuer-configurable expiry (hours), and revocation.
6. **Farm credential.** It is a separate key, registered only after the key swap succeeds, with the same custody as the admin key.
7. **Default manager.** The concept is dropped. The claimed VTA becomes the active agent unless the proposal's author shows a further need.
8. **No recovery.** The first release accepts it and says so plainly on the claim screen. Optional recovery is planned as a follow-up.
9. **Key custody.** Hardware custody does not block the first release. It ships on the Credo KMS, hardware custody is tracked separately, and curve support for Ed25519 is checked first.
10. **Verification.** Before building against the Farm contract, run `scripts/openvtc/setup-external.mjs` and obtain `vtafarm-api`. Check free memory first.

**Order.** Settle 3, 1 and 4 with the Farm owners first. Then do 6 and the small wallet work. Items 8 and 9 follow as follow-ups.
