## 1. R5 and R7, as measured

The reasoning and the order the blockers were found in are in
[`2026-09-20-al.md`](./2026-09-20-al.md) §12–§16. What that companion does not
record, because it landed after it was written:

- **The phone ↔ VTA leg runs on Rev 3 too.** The manager's `did:peer` key was
  minted in the wallet's own KMS, so one Ed25519 key backs both TSP ports
  (converted to X25519 at use, as `did:peer:2` derives its agreement key). No new
  key material and no change to enrolment. Measured on a passing run: one
  greeting to the VTA, then six tasks over Rev 3, each depending on the last.
- **The peer's accept is now received and released.** Once the VTC could answer
  our invite, each `XRFA` hit a codec that refused control frames by name; the
  refusal skipped the acknowledgement and the accept stayed queued at the
  mediator, charged to the VTC. That is VTI-29's shape, caused by us. Fixed by
  reporting a control frame as "no Trust Task here" rather than by acknowledging
  everything, since a failed persist must still withhold its ack.

So R5 stands at *the send-only introduction upstream's services use*, and R7 at
*green in the lab for the ecosystem legs*. `XRFD`, the invite race, the
applicant ↔ vetter leg, a >12,285-byte message and the Farm run stay open.
