# 2026-09-07 — Alberto

Disposition on [`2026-09-04-bm.md`](./2026-09-04-bm.md) and the §1 paragraph it
produced, written during the pre-Prague branch consolidation.

## The non-core constraint is adopted as written

Brendan's ask — that VTA Farm support be *"valuable but fully optional, and
must not become a core dependency"* of `openvtc-integration-plan.md`,
`pnm_cnm_subtask.md` or `reference-app-sdk-packaging.md` — is correct and is
adopted without amendment. The §3.4 rework is right too: this plan's exposure
to the `did:webvh` adapter's absence is a fact about this plan, not a licence
to move the adapter's priority on the parent's roadmap. `ref-06x-cypress-stack`
proved that need against our own self-hosted VTA with no Farm involved.

Nothing in that paragraph is being walked back here.

## What it does not say, and now does

The paragraph answers "what may this plan block?" with *nothing*. Read on its
own — particularly by someone arriving at this document cold, months from now —
it also reads as an answer to "how much does this matter?", which it was never
meant to be, and the answer it appears to give is wrong.

**The Farm is the primary interoperability target for Prague** — the OSS Linux
Foundation Summit Europe, 2026-10-01. Geoff has provisioned access, and it is
the venue where Keyring is expected to exchange with infrastructure it does not
run. F0–F2 are therefore schedule-critical against a fixed external date.

These two facts are not in tension, and the added paragraph says so explicitly
rather than leaving a reader to reconcile them:

- **Non-blocking** is a claim about the dependency graph. If the Farm work
  slips, no phase of the three sibling plans stalls.
- **Time-boxed** is a claim about the calendar. If the Farm work slips, Prague
  is what is lost.

A document that records only the first invites a future reader to deprioritise
the work on the grounds that nothing depends on it. That inference is available
today and would be wrong today, which is the definition of a gap worth closing
in the plan text rather than in someone's memory.

## Position on our own VTA, unchanged

Prague running on the Farm does not retire the standalone VTA. The Farm is a
managed service someone else operates; the ability to stand up our own instance
remains a distinct capability the lab wants for its own infrastructure, and the
sequencing is that the standalone VTA is a rung toward the Farm rather than an
alternative to it. `ref-05-local-vta` staying the CI default (§6) already
encodes this correctly and should stay that way.
