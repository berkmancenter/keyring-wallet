## Part B. Mediated proving and PR #114

AL-24 N4 asked whether Phase 4 satisfies the zero-knowledge plan's rule that mediated proving "MUST NOT be the only path available to a holder".

**What was read.** `plan/zero-knowledge` at `1c399f57` (2026-10-15), `docs/plans/zero-knowledge-plan.md`. Its constraint C6-b quotes the sentence from `trustoverip/dtgwg-zkp-spec` (Accessibility Considerations), and marks the section informative. The specification was read here only through that plan's quotation.

**Reasoning.**
1. The rule's subject is a holder who "delegates proving to an agent". DBI Phase 3 has the holder's VTA prove, so it is mediated proving. A holder-controlled VTA (§5.2) narrows who the agent is, not whether proving is delegated.
2. While Phase 3 is the only path, the rule is not met. That follows from the sentence and needs no reading of intent.
3. Phase 4 meets it only if it ships. The plan made Phase 4 optional ("starts only if Phase 3 leaves a reason to prove off the server"), so the plan did not guarantee a second path. The phone-side figures that exist are for the stock circuit; the custom circuit's estimated 0.7–2.1 GiB may not fit, so Phase 4 can fail on its own terms, and then the rule could not be met by this design at all.
4. Whether the rule binds is the part that could not be settled. The section is informative, #114 itself records the sentence's normative status as open, and the rule is written in the setting of hidden vetting and Keyring's own credentials rather than a state credential's presentation proof. Guessing either way would have turned an undecided point into plan text.

**Disposition.** Q28 records the reasoning, the three options (binding, advisory, satisfied another way) and what each makes a gate, owned by Brendan with the author of #114. Phase 4's start condition names Q28.

**An observation about #114, not edited here.** AL-24 N4 says #114 "runs no prover" and moved hidden-vetting proving to the VTA on 10-14. At `1c399f57` the file is not consistent on that: its status line and §3.1 say the VTA proves for the applicant (D4, decided 2026-10-14), while §3.1.2 says the phone path is built first (decided 2026-10-11, "Keyring builds the on-phone applicant path"). The plan therefore cites #114 as a cross-reference and names the inconsistency in Q28, rather than relying on either reading.
