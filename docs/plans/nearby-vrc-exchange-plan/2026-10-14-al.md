# 2026-10-14 — The product owner's answers on the offline carriage and the witness hand-off

Companion to [`../nearby-vrc-exchange-plan.md`](../nearby-vrc-exchange-plan.md).
It records two decisions the product owner made on the plan's open questions, and how
the plan applies them. It is not revised afterwards.

## Decision 1 — the offline carriage is wanted, but optional

**Asked:** should a nearby exchange work with no network at all? That is Phase 3,
carrying the whole ceremony over Bluetooth with a `ble-gatt` carriage.

**Answer:** "it should, but not necessary".

**Applied:**

- Phase 3 is renamed "optional, later". It comes after Phases 0–2, and none of them
  depends on it.
- The question is removed from §7.
- §1.3 calls it an optional later phase rather than a separate decision.

**Supersedes:** the 2026-10-11 position, in which Phase 3 was conditional on a yes or
no from the product owner. The open costs recorded then still stand, and they belong
to the phase's own subtask when it is opened:

- a new carriage;
- chunked Trust Task documents;
- a contact that cannot be messaged until both phones are online.

## Decision 2 — Nearby hands off to the witnessed ceremony when a witness is available

**Asked:** when both people also have a witness available, does Nearby hand off to the
witnessed ceremony, or stay unwitnessed?

**Answer:** yes, it hands off. That is the option the plan recommended.

**Applied:**

- **New §4.1.** The code confirmation and the session binding run first. Then `propose`
  carries `witnessed: true` alongside `ext.nearby.sessionDigest`, and each party runs
  its own `witness/session`.
- **The badge.** The witness's result decides it: "Confirmed in person" only when the
  witness confirms locality. A failed witness falls back to "Exchanged nearby".
- **One advert at a time.** The phone stops the nearby advert before the witness's
  locality advert starts.
- **Tests.** Phase 1 gains a build item and a Jest acceptance case, and §6 gains a
  real-device check for the hand-off.
- **Scope line.** It now reads "No witness is needed; when one is available, the
  exchange hands off".
- **§7.** The question is removed.

**Why the binding is kept under a witness.** The digest check is what ties the network
ceremony to the connection the two people confirmed by eye. Dropping it when a witness
is present would let a witnessed ceremony start on a connection nobody confirmed. The
witness attests to its own observation; it does not authenticate the connection
between the two phones.

**Unchanged by this decision.** Without a witness, the plan still makes no co-presence
claim (`locality-plan.md` §2.1). With one, any such claim is the witness's alone, made
under the locality plan.
