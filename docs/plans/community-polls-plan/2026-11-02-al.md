# 2 November 2026: upstream voting research for Keyring

Parent: [../community-polls-plan.md](../community-polls-plan.md).

Read from source and docs on 2 November, at VTI main 3139e00e and vta-browser-plugin main
f89796b1. Nothing was run, and no request went to First VTC or the lab.

## Findings

1. **Voting is real and recent upstream.** `vti-voting`, `vti-voting-service`, `vtc/elections/*` and
   `voting/*` all exist. The docs' status line says it runs end to end in tests, but hasn't run on
   deployed infrastructure. A maintainer said on 1 November that First VTC can be tested on, so
   that line may be behind.
2. **A voter needs VTA 0.69 or newer.** Paths under `vta/pcs/voter` and `vta/pcs/vote`: 0 at
   vta-service-v0.67.0, 13 at v0.69.0. Our 0.67 agents can't vote, including the version
   recommended for rehearsal agents on 1 November. v0.69.1 was tagged on 2 November.
3. **VTC 0.17.0 lacks the newest voting work.** #2364 (guided builder), #2367 (portal voting), #2370
   (trusted box list) and #2371 are after vtc-service-v0.17.0 and in v0.17.1. That matches the
   lab's New election page: a free-text box DID and a sample manifest.
4. **`voting` is a VTC build feature, off by default** (`vtc-service/Cargo.toml`, line 55, not in
   `default`). The console's Elections page doesn't prove a VTC serves it. Whether Farm builds
   include it is unknown.
5. **A community needs a trusted box in host config** (`[[voting.trusted_ballot_boxes]]`), and the box
   must allow the community (`[communities] allowed`). A maintainer offered to add a test VTC.
6. **The browser plugin is the working client to copy.** Commits a649b100 and f89796b1 show the real
   order of tasks, and rules Keyring should keep: the pause of up to 20 s, the receipt check, a
   stored "you voted" with no choice, and asking for the box's address before use.
7. **PCS isn't a level.** Blind tickets and one-time cast keys apply at `open`, `sealed` and
   `tallied-encrypted`. Secrecy levels decide who can read ballots.
8. **The member's agent sees the choice.** `vta/pcs/vote/0.2` takes the ballot in its payload. It's
   the member's own agent, but a hosted agent's host is then in that trust. The app must not imply
   otherwise.
9. **Upstream calls the ballot cryptography unaudited,** "fine for polls and surveys; have it
   reviewed before a binding election" (`docs/07-voting/README.md`, status note).

## Not checked
